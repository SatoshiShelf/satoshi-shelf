# 30 Short-Form Video Scripts (faceless)

**Format for every script:** text-on-screen + voiceover (your voice or a free AI voice) over stock b-roll / screen recordings. No camera, no face.

**Make each one like this:**
- **Hook (0–3s):** big text on screen + spoken. This decides everything.
- **Beats:** 3–5 quick lines, one per screen, ~2–3s each. Keep text short.
- **CTA:** send them to your **bio link** (the free guide): `https://xghabra.github.io/satoshi-shelf/free.html`
- **Bio link + pinned comment** carry the link. Never say a raw URL out loud.

**Post:** 1–2/day to TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels (same file everywhere).
**Rule:** only every 4th–5th video pushes the product. The rest give value or push the free guide.
**Hashtags:** mix broad + niche, e.g. #sidehustle #makemoneyonline #digitalproducts #freelance #passiveincome #smallbusiness #ai #bitcoin

## How to film without a camera (CapCut, free)
1. Pick a script. Record the voiceover (your voice or AI) — or skip voiceover and use text-on-screen + music.
2. Drop in b-roll: free stock (Pexels/Pixabay) or screen recordings of the product.
3. Add big text, one line per screen, changing every 2–3s.
4. Add captions (auto-caption) — most people watch muted.
5. Export in 9:16, post to all three apps.

## The content mix that converts
- 4–5 value/entertainment videos
- 1 that mentions the free guide
- Occasionally 1 that pitches a product directly
- Pin the free-guide link in the top comment on every video

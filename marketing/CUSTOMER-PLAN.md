## Step 1 — Build the audience you own (ongoing, free)

The email list is the only channel no algorithm can take away.

1. Pick one lead magnet. You already have the **7-Day Digital Product Kickstart** — use it.
2. Put the signup on every page. Offer something *extra* for signing up (e.g. "join to get the 14-day launch calendar" — that's your `first-100-sprint` cut down).
3. Send a welcome email the second they join, then 2–3 more over the first week pointing to your best free stuff. Sell only after you've given value twice.

**Target:** 100 subscribers before you push hard on paid. A list of 100 beats 10,000 anonymous views.

## Step 2 — Pick exactly TWO traffic channels

Do **not** spread across ten platforms. Two, done consistently. Recommended pair for your products:

### Channel A — Pinterest (evergreen search traffic) ✅ best fit
- Pins keep sending visitors for years, and the audience actively looks for "how to make money / templates / side hustle."
- Create a free business account, 5–8 boards (e.g. "Side Hustle Ideas", "Freelance Tips", "Make Money Online", "Digital Products").
- Design 3–5 pins per product with Canva free. Pin daily-ish using a scheduler.
- Every pin links to the matching product page or the free guide.
- Use your **Pinterest Traffic Playbook** as the manual.

### Channel B — Short-form video (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels) ✅ highest reach
- Faceless: screen recordings, text-on-screen, stock b-roll, AI voice. No camera needed.
- Use your **Faceless Content Machine** as the manual.
- Post 1–2/day. Every 4th–5th video mentions the free guide; the bio links to it.
- One video that lands can out-earn weeks of posting.

**Why not Reddit as the main channel?** Reddit is high-trust but hostile to self-promo and easily bans you (you already got one). Use it for *value comments only*, with your profile linking the store — never as your primary engine.

## Step 3 — The content formula that converts

For every piece of content, follow this shape:

```
Hook (pain)  →  3 quick tips  →  "want the full thing? free guide in bio"
```

- The tips give real value → earns attention.
- The free guide captures the email → builds the list.
- The product is the "done-for-you" shortcut → converts the buyers.

Never lead with "buy my product." Lead with the problem.

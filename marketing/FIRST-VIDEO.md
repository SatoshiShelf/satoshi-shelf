## How to make it in CapCut (free, ~15 min)

1. New project → set ratio **9:16**.
2. Voiceover: tap **Text → Text to speech** on each line (free AI voice) OR record your own in a quiet room. Easiest = text-to-speech.
3. B-roll: download 3–5 free clips from **Pexels** or **Pixabay** (search: laptop, typing, spreadsheet, phone). Drop one behind each beat.
4. Add each on-screen line as a big **text** layer, one per beat. Bold font, high contrast.
5. **Auto-captions** (captions button) — always on.
6. Add music at low volume (CapCut has a free library).
7. Export **1080p, 30fps**.

> No voiceover at all? It still works: text-on-screen + music only. Do what gets it posted.

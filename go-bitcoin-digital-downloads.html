Questions

Bitcoin buying, answered plainly

Which wallets work?

Any Bitcoin wallet that can scan a QR code or send to a bc1 address. No Lightning support or channel setup needed.

Do I need to hold a whole Bitcoin?

No. Products start at $1.99 and checkout converts that to sats at the live rate. You pay the Bitcoin network fee separately.

Is the price fixed in USD?

Yes. The USD price is fixed and converted at checkout, so pay the amount shown.

What if my payment is still pending?

On-chain payments usually appear in the mempool within minutes. The store accepts a mempool sighting at the exact amount, and confirmation follows shortly.

Is my payment private?

There's no email, card or personal data collected. On-chain payments are public on the blockchain: the store can see the amount, address and timing, but does not tie them to an identity.

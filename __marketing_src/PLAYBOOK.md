**Rules:** read each sub's rules. Many ban links; put the link in a comment if asked, or say "free guide in my profile." Always answer questions for a few days.

Hook in the first 2 seconds. Show the result first, then how. Put the link in bio.

## Rules to not get banned

- Never drop a link in your first comment in a new community.
- 10 helpful comments per 1 link.
- No fake scarcity, no fake testimonials, no "guaranteed money."
- Disclose it's yours.
- Reply to every reply for the first 48 hours after a post.

## The honest truth

Traffic is the hard part. Expect 1–2 weeks of consistent effort
before meaningful clicks, and 2–4 weeks before meaningful sales.
Volume and consistency beat cleverness. The people who win are the
ones who keep posting helpful things after everyone else quits.

# Getting A LOT of clients — the volume plan

**The truth about volume:** there is no switch that floods you with buyers. Volume comes from running several small channels at once, consistently, and — most importantly — **borrowing audiences that already exist** instead of waiting for strangers to find you. A brand-new store with no traffic gets zero clients, no matter how good the products are.

## Start with the math (so expectations are real)

Typical cold funnel: **1,000 views → ~30 clicks (3%) → ~3 signups (10%) → ~0.3 sales.**
So "a lot of sales" means either:
- appearing where thousands of **buyers** already shop (marketplaces), or
- **evergreen** traffic that compounds (Pinterest/SEO), or
- **broad reach** (short-form video).

Running one channel = a trickle. Running three = a flow. That's the whole plan.

---

## Tier 1 — Marketplaces (borrow existing buyers) — FASTEST to volume

This is the #1 move for "a lot of clients." Marketplaces already have millions of shoppers searching for exactly what you sell. You bring the product; they bring the traffic.

| Where | Best for | Fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | digital downloads (guides, templates) | ~6.5% + listing | Huge buyer intent for "printables/planners/guides" |
| **Gumroad** | any digital product | 10% | Has its own Discover feed |
| **Payhip** | digital products | 5% | Simple, free plan |
| **Ko-fi** | digital + tips | ~5% | Also gives you cards/PayPal payouts |
| **Lemon Squeezy** | digital products | ~5% | Merchant of record, handles tax |
| **Fiverr / Upwork** | your **services** (see below) | ~20% | Clients, not just customers |
| **Pinterest** | not a store, but a traffic pump | free | See Tier 2 |

**Do this:** create accounts on **Etsy + Gumroad + Payhip**, upload the same PDFs, and write proper titles/tags. Now your products are searchable by people who are already in buying mode. This can produce more sales than your whole website in the first month.

> Trade-off: marketplaces take a cut and you don't own the customer. That's fine — you're buying *volume and proof*. Keep your own Bitcoin store for the direct (fee-free) buyers.

## Tier 2 — Pinterest (free, evergreen, compounding)

Pinterest is a search engine, and your niche (make money / side hustles / freelance / templates) is enormous there.
- Free business account. 8–10 boards (Side Hustle Ideas, Make Money Online, Freelance Tips, Digital Products, Passive Income...).
- Design 3–5 pins per product (Canva free). Keywords in the pin title + description.
- Post 5–10 pins/day (schedule with the free Tailwind or Pinterest's own scheduler).
- Every pin links to the matching product page or the free guide.
- Pins keep pulling traffic for **years**. This is the single best free long-term engine.
- Manual: your **Pinterest Traffic Playbook**.

## Tier 3 — Short-form video (highest reach, free)

- Faceless: screen recordings, text-on-screen, stock b-roll, AI voice. No camera.
- Post 1–3/day to **TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels** (same video everywhere).
- Every 4th–5th video mentions the free guide; bio links to the free guide → email list → product.
- 1 video that lands can beat a month of anything else.
- Manual: your **Faceless Content Machine**.

## Tier 4 — SEO pages on your own site (compounding)

You have 18 products = 18 chances to rank. Create one plain "blog" page per product targeting a **long-tail** phrase someone types when they're stuck, e.g.:
- "how to make your first $100 online"
- "how to write a freelance proposal"
- "how to price a digital product"
- "how to get paid online without a business account"

One page: the problem, a genuinely useful answer, then a soft link to the product. Add these to the site + sitemap. Slow to start, but it compounds forever and costs nothing.

---

## Tier 5 — Communities (trust, careful)

Answer questions where your buyer already is: **Quora, relevant subreddits, Facebook groups, Discords, niche forums.**
- Give complete, useful answers with **no link**.
- Put the link in your profile / mention it only when asked.
- Never spam; you already saw how fast that gets you banned.
- 3–5 great answers a day beats one promotional post.

## The repurposing engine (work once, publish everywhere)

From **one** idea ("how to make your first $100 online") produce:
1. A Pinterest pin set (3–5 images)
2. One short-form video
3. One tweet/X thread
4. One Quora/Reddit answer
5. One SEO blog page on your site
6. One email to your list

Six assets from one idea. This is how you actually sustain daily posting without burning out.

## Services = clients with much bigger wallets

Products bring volume; **services bring money fast**. Offer a done-for-you version:
- "I'll set up a Bitcoin storefront for you — $79"
- "I'll turn your idea into a sellable digital product — $99"
- "I'll write 10 cold outreach messages for your business — $49"

Two or three service clients at $79 beats 20 product sales. List these on **Fiverr/Upwork** and mention them on your product pages as an upsell.
